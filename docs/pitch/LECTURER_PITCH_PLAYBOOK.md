# Mframapa Denmark pitch — 5 slides, animation-heavy

**One deck. Max 5 slides. ~2 minutes spoken.**

Mock-audience (lecturer) feedback is **not** more slides. It is **motion inside these five**: builds, timelines, photo cycles, phone walkthrough. Spoken words stay sparse; the PPTX does the teaching.

Script base: `DENMARK_SUMMIT_2MIN_SCRIPT.md`  
Brand tokens: **light only** — canvas `#F6F7F9` / white, ink `#0A0A0A`, mint `#00C896`, Syne + DM Sans. No night/black slide backgrounds.  
File: `Mframapa_Smart_Air_Quality_Advisor_Denmark.pptx`  
QR → `https://mframapa.live` on **every** slide (corner). Title line: **Mframapa — Smart Air Quality Advisor**

---

## Honesty (on slide + voice)

- Daily **city estimates**, not street sensors  
- Satellite + weather + AI → reading → **What to do**  
- No funding ask, no fake traction, no live-demo dependency

## The 5 slides (what to build)

### Slide 1 — Breath / problem  
**BG:** Light canvas / white.

**Static**

- Logo top left  
- Huge dark ink center: `What did you just breathe in?`  
- Tiny: `mframapa`

**Animation invest (10–15s of builds while Frances opens)**

1. Atmosphere photo fades in (framed or soft-scrimmed on light BG — keep slide light)  
2. Line appears under: `You cannot see whether it is safe.`  
3. Optional second beat: mint icons (eye · cough · breath) pulse once, then settle — **consequences teaser**, not a lecture  

**Spoken:** breath hook + invisible problem (~0:00–0:20)

---

### Slide 2 — Stakes + people  
**BG:** Canvas.

**Headline:** `When the air is dirty, people pay.`

**Animation invest (this slide carries Phases 5 + 7)**

**Act A — Consequences (auto or click 1–2)**

- Left cluster builds: immediate (eyes, headache, cough, asthma)  
- Right timeline draws: `months` → `1 year` → `lungs / heart`  
- Almost no body text; icons + 3 timeline labels  

**Act B — Stakeholders (click or morph)**

- Same slide crossfades or wipes to **6 photo tiles**: students, children, elders, outdoor workers, clinics, communities  
- Or: consequence icons shrink to a strip; photo grid fills the stage  

**Spoken:** one sentence on consequences + “families, schools, clinics, outdoor workers” (~0:20–0:45). Let the grid speak.

---

### Slide 3 — Story → advisor  
**BG:** Canvas. **This is the product + AI + remedies slide.**

**Headline:** `Know today’s air. Know what to do.`

**Animation invest (Phases 4, 6, 8, 9 — heaviest craft)**

**Act A — Story (3 beats, ~1s each)**

1. Panel: student walking / campus path  
2. Panel: phone warning / poor air  
3. Panel: follows advice (mask / indoors / shorter walk)  

**Act B — Phone takes over (embed MP4 or stepped screenshots)**

- Phone frame center; play **silent** recording: open city → category → **What to do**  
- Overlay callouts that animate on:  
  - `Interprets the reading`  
  - `Flags who is at risk`  
  - `Tells you what to do`  

**Act C — Remedies strip (builds under phone)**

Short chips only: `Ease off outside` · `Shorter play for kids` · `Mask if you must go` · `Keep windows wise`

**Spoken:** what mframapa is + AI as health advisor + 1–2 remedies (~0:45–1:20). Do **not** narrate every chip.

---

### Slide 4 — How + proof  
**BG:** Light canvas / white.

**Headline:** `Built in Accra. We look up.`

**Animation invest (Phases 9–12)**

**Act A — Pipeline (motion diagram)**

```
Satellite + weather  →  AI models  →  City estimate  →  What to do
```

Each node lights in sequence; thin mint arrows. Under: one honesty line: `Estimates from space, made useful on the ground.`

**Act B — Field evidence (same slide, second half)**

- Strip or 2×2 of **real sites**: photo + tiny app crop + time  
- Map pin pulse optional  
- Caption: `Tested in real places` (not a data dump)

**Spoken:** how it works (look up / train on stations / city estimate) + “we checked real places” (~1:20–1:50). Point at one photo; don’t list all.

---

### Slide 5 — Close  
**BG:** Light canvas / white (same as slides 1–4).

**Static**

- Center dark ink: `You cannot protect what you cannot see.`  
- Subtitle: `Mframapa — Smart Air Quality Advisor`  
- **Large QR** + `mframapa.live`  
- Logo  

**Animation invest**

- Line fades up; QR scales in; optional soft mint wash  
- Keep still for the last 5–10s so people can scan  

**Spoken:** honesty + find me after + thank you (~1:50–2:00)

## Design rules

- Max **5** slides, 16:9  
- Huge headlines; fragments only  
- **Builds > bullet lists**  
- One idea per *act*, not per wall of text  
- Footer: mark · Smart Air Quality Advisor · QR  
- Bans: purple AI kitsch, emoji, reading the slide, fragile live wifi demo
